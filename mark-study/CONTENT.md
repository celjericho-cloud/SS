## Preservation and condensation

Finished teaching points and their applications are condensed into connected notes. The site preserves the source’s Trinitarian teaching, identification of Jesus with Yahweh, salvation by faith rather than human effort, repentance, discipleship, interpretation of the Sabbath, and its treatment of blasphemy against the Holy Ghost. The main Gospel applications are retained rather than replaced by general self-improvement advice. Historical context is labeled separately. Cross-references can be expanded to read their KJV text.
