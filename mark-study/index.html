ABOUT THIS STUDY

From the classroom
to the page.

These 16 lessons preserve expanded teaching outlines from the completed teaching material in the 69-page Book of Mark Series PDF. Repeated export blocks, most preliminary observations, private anecdotes, and research notes have been omitted. The main teaching flow retains explanations, class questions, applications, and closing challenges. Concise verse connections provide an additional reference beneath it. The teacher’s theological framing and terminology are retained.

Source page numbers accompany each lesson. Titles follow the finished material; passage order places the storm lesson in Mark 4 and the separate devotional in Mark 12. Mark 4:34 and 6:30 are included where the teaching uses them. Overview history is distinguished from the biblical text.

Verse connections are editorial mappings of the teacher’s insights and applications to the text. Applications draw principles from these passages; they are not additional Scripture. Cross-references are shown separately.

KJV text is bundled locally from thiagobodruk/bible’s KJV dataset. No external services or tracking are used. Display preferences are saved on this device when browser storage is available.
