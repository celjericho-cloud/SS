The feeding lesson preserves the manuscript’s three-part argument, the distinction between Christ’s sufficiency and guaranteed freedom from suffering, the challenge to seek satisfaction in Christ, and the closing Psalm 23 reading. Repeated export blocks are consolidated. Its prose is adapted into teaching bullets rather than replaced with a short summary.
